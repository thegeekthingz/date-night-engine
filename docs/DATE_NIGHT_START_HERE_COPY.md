# Start your date

**START YOUR DATE → https://date-night-game-1.netlify.app**

Access the interactive Date Night experience using the link provided with your purchase.

1. Choose your vibe: Cozy, Funny, Romantic or Competitive.
2. Choose 15 or 30 minutes (a pacing guide; go at your own pace).
3. Choose Sweet or Flirty.
4. Build your date.
5. Follow the five activities: Connect, Predict, Play, Challenge and Closer.

Open the link in your browser with internet access. No app-store download, installation, account or login is required. Reveal shows the comparison instructions where available; Swap offers another eligible activity in the same stage.

Progress saves locally in the same browser on the same device when localStorage is available. Refreshing restores the saved date. There is no cloud sync; clearing browser data or using private browsing may remove the save. Offline WebApp support is not claimed.
