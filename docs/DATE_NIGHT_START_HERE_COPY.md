## HOW IT WORKS

1. **Choose your vibe:** Cozy, Funny, Romantic or Competitive.
2. **Choose your time:** Quick Date — about 15 minutes; Full Date — about 30 minutes.
3. **Choose your intensity:** Sweet or Flirty.
4. **Build your date:** the WebApp creates a guided five-activity date: Connect → Predict → Play → Challenge → Closer.
5. **Go at your own pace:** the time options are guidance, not a countdown.

## YOUR INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCE

- 72 activities
- 4 vibes
- About 15 or 30 minute options
- Sweet or Flirty
- Dynamically composed dates
- Reveal on eligible activities
- Swap when an eligible replacement is available
- Browser-local session saving

Activities may appear again across different sessions. Flirty can include Sweet activities too.

## SAVE / RESTORE NOTE

Your current Date Night session is saved in the browser on the device you are using when browser storage is available. Return using the same browser and device. It does not sync automatically between devices. Clearing browser data or using private browsing may remove saved progress.
