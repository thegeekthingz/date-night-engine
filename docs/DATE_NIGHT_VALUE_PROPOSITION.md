# Date Night - Product value and expectation copy

This is reusable product explanation, not an Etsy listing or launch asset. It states current features without promising undefined access terms.

## The main experience: interactive WebApp

**One guided date. Five activities. Zero planning.**

Choose one of four vibes - Cozy, Funny, Romantic or Competitive - a 15- or 30-minute pacing option, and Sweet or Flirty. The WebApp builds a complete five-stage date from its 72-activity library before you begin: Connect, Predict, Play, Challenge and Closer.

- **Dynamic combinations:** receive a complete route instead of selecting five separate prompts yourself.
- **Reveal:** show the comparison content on activities that support it, when you are ready; it does not advance the date.
- **Swap:** request a different eligible activity for the current stage without changing progress. Availability depends on remaining content and the selected vibe, intensity and duration budget; unlimited replacements are not promised.
- **Save and return:** restore the saved date in the same browser/profile when local storage is available. Browser data clearing or privacy restrictions can affect saves. No account or device sync is implied.

The WebApp runs in a browser; no app-store download is required. Use an internet connection to open/reload it. Access URL, duration and any commercial terms must be defined before launch; do not substitute a temporary development link.

## The bonus: offline printable mini-dates

Four ready-to-play, fixed dates for two - Cozy, Funny, Romantic and Competitive - with 20 selected activities in total. Each page includes all five stages and its Predict comparison instruction. Print one date page and put the phone away.

A4 and US Letter each come in Color and Ink-Saver. These are four editions of the **same** mini-dates, not additional activities. Static PDFs are not editable templates and do not include all 72 WebApp items. They are a practical offline backup, not a printable clone of the interactive product.

## Replay copy

“The WebApp dynamically composes dates from a finite library of 72 activities. Activities may return on a different date, while the same item is not intentionally repeated within one composed session. The printable bonus offers four fixed offline dates that you can revisit with different answers.”

No unlimited-unique-session or never-repeat claim. Swap also excludes queued, displayed and retired IDs within the current session; there is no persistent cross-date history.

## Flirty copy

“Sweet keeps things warm, playful and easygoing. Flirty adds room for a little more spark: tasteful, teasing and non-explicit. A Flirty date can include both Sweet and Flirty activities; it does not guarantee a Flirty item every time.”

Do not imply explicit adult content, a required level of intimacy or a quiet/no-performance filter. The Romantic printable keeps its Flirty identity with one chemistry scene followed by a quieter guessing challenge.

## Time copy

“Choose a quick or fuller date with the 15- and 30-minute pacing options. These are estimates, not a timer: go at your own pace.”
