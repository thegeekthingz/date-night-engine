# Date Night - Printable content map (revision 2)

Four fixed offline journeys, five activities each, drawn from 20 distinct production IDs. Source game version 2.0.0; printable revision 2 is a content-only correction with the same page architecture.

## Cozy Date

From a small comfort to an imaginary shared haven, then a gentle celebration and a memory to keep.

### CONNECT - DN-CON-001

**Vibe:** cozy | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Finish this sentence in turn: “A tiny thing that helps me unwind is…” Choose one you could share on an ordinary evening.

**Reason selected:** An easy, low-pressure opening grounded in everyday comfort.

### PREDICT - DN-PRE-001

**Vibe:** cozy | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Picture your partner’s ideal end to a busy day: quiet company, a long chat or something to laugh at. Choose their answer before asking them.

**Now compare your answers:** Say your guesses first. Each reveal your choice and the small detail that would make it feel just right.

**Reason selected:** Discovers what helps each person settle after a busy day.

### PLAY - DN-PLY-007

**Vibe:** cozy | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Design an imaginary room made for the two of you. Build it aloud in four rounds: comfort, delight, quiet and one impossible feature; spend a little time exploring each other’s additions.

**Reason selected:** A collaborative centerpiece that builds on the idea of comfort.

### CHALLENGE - DN-CHA-003

**Vibe:** cozy | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Create the smallest possible celebration using only your hands. Teach it to each other and perform your two versions together.

**Reason selected:** Shifts from talking to a small, gentle shared gesture.

### CLOSER - DN-CLO-001

**Vibe:** cozy | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Make a spoken postcard from tonight: each choose a moment, a detail and a feeling you would put on it. Share your versions with each other and end with one line you would keep.

**Reason selected:** Collects details from this particular date into an intentional ending.

## Funny Date

Warm up with an everyday story, get wonderfully impractical, try a tiny debate and finish with your own sign-off.

### CONNECT - DN-CON-002

**Vibe:** funny | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Give today a made-up episode title. Trade the story behind your titles, including the detail that would make the trailer.

**Reason selected:** Turns an ordinary day into a light opening story.

### PREDICT - DN-PRE-002

**Vibe:** funny | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** An imaginary remote can mute hiccups, summon a perfect snack or translate animal thoughts. Which button would your partner press first?

**Now compare your answers:** Reveal your choices together. Explain the first situation in which you would use your button.

**Reason selected:** Creates a specific absurd choice with an immediate comparison.

### PLAY - DN-PLY-002

**Vibe:** funny | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Invent a useless household gadget together. Give it a name, a ridiculous feature and a sales pitch delivered by both of you.

**Reason selected:** Makes something together rather than only answering questions.

### CHALLENGE - DN-CHA-008

**Vibe:** funny | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Hold three tiny debates: square pillows versus round pillows, whispering robots versus singing doors, and pockets versus capes. Each gets about a minute to make a case per round; change sides for the last one.

**Reason selected:** Adds a performance/rivalry energy shift with harmless subjects.

### CLOSER - DN-CLO-003

**Vibe:** funny | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Invent a short sign-off for tonight using a detail you each enjoyed. Trade the stories behind your choices, combine them into one phrase and choose a playful or quiet way to say it together to end the date.

**Reason selected:** Ends with a shared phrase that belongs to this date.

## Romantic Date

Notice attraction in small details, discover anticipation, create a scene, guess a tempting destination and finish with an ending you both welcome.

### CONNECT - DN-CON-015

**Vibe:** romantic | **Intensity:** flirty

**Printable wording:** Trade three tiny details that make you feel drawn to your partner: a look, a habit or a way they do something ordinary. Take turns telling the small story behind each one and leave time to hear what they noticed about you.

**Reason selected:** Starts with specific attraction rather than forced vulnerability.

### PREDICT - DN-PRE-013

**Vibe:** romantic | **Intensity:** flirty

**Printable wording:** Predict your partner’s favourite kind of anticipation: a playful clue, a direct invitation or a surprise detail. Think of a tasteful example for your guess.

**Now compare your answers:** Reveal your real preferences. Explain which detail makes anticipation fun for you, then compare your examples.

**Reason selected:** Makes anticipation personal without explicit content.

### PLAY - DN-PLY-013

**Vibe:** romantic | **Intensity:** flirty

**Printable wording:** Build a scene with obvious chemistry and no romantic words. Choose a setting, improvise a short exchange, then change who makes the first move in the scene.

**Reason selected:** Creates a short shared scene, adding action and chemistry.

### CHALLENGE - DN-CHA-013

**Vibe:** romantic | **Intensity:** flirty

**Printable wording:** Describe an imaginary date destination using only five words. Your partner gets three guesses before you reveal it; exchange roles and make the second round a little more tempting.

**Reason selected:** A five-word destination guessing game shifts from the chemistry scene to playful discovery without a second delivery performance.

### CLOSER - DN-CLO-006

**Vibe:** romantic | **Intensity:** flirty

**Printable wording:** Choose one small way to end with a little more spark: a warm compliment, a held gaze or an offered hand. Each suggest your preference and choose an ending you both welcome.

**Reason selected:** Ends with mutual choice and gentle, optional affection.

## Competitive Date

Recognize each other’s strengths, test predictions, solve an invented mystery and try a shared memory challenge before choosing another date.

### CONNECT - DN-CON-004

**Vibe:** competitive | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Choose one everyday talent you admire in each other. Explain where you would recruit your partner to use it on an imaginary two-person team.

**Reason selected:** Frames the couple as a team before the playful rivalry.

### PREDICT - DN-PRE-010

**Vibe:** competitive | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** In three rounds, predict which your partner would rather master: cooking or storytelling, fixing things or finding things, planning or improvising. Give a reason for each guess.

**Now compare your answers:** Reveal your actual choices one pair at a time. Test one prediction with a tiny demonstration of the skill you picked.

**Reason selected:** Tests specific guesses without right/wrong relationship judgments.

### PLAY - DN-PLY-010

**Vibe:** competitive | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Solve a mystery you invent together: why is there a tiny door behind the sofa? Alternate adding clues, discuss two possible explanations and agree on a final reveal.

**Reason selected:** An interactive problem to solve collaboratively, with no scoring.

### CHALLENGE - DN-CHA-005

**Vibe:** competitive | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Build a sound sequence together: one person makes a sound, the other repeats it and adds one. Keep extending it at an easy pace, then try a second sequence with roles reversed.

**Reason selected:** Adds a repeat-and-build challenge with natural turns and no loser.

### CLOSER - DN-CLO-004

**Vibe:** competitive | **Intensity:** sweet

**Printable wording:** Each offer one tiny, no-cost date you could share at home another time. Pick the idea you are both most curious about and end by inviting each other to it.

**Reason selected:** Directs the energy toward one mutually chosen next date.
